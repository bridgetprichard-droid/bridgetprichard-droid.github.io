Articulate Rise course

Creating Accessible Patient Education Materials

I designed this course to help healthcare professionals create patient education materials people can find, understand, and use.

The course covers barriers to understanding, plain language, and inclusive formats. Learners apply those principles to examples and check their understanding with a quiz.

What to look for

A before-and-after comparison shows learners how to rewrite patient instructions in plain language. The quiz asks them to apply the same principle to a new example.
